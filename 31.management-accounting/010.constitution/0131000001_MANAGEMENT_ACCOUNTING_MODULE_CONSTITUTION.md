## 1. Purpose
The Management Accounting module governs internal profitability and managerial allocation truth.
It is the canonical module for cost center / segment / managerial profit view canon,
internal allocation results, and management performance analysis truth.

## 2. Canonical scope
The module owns:
- management allocation rule canon
- cost center result canon
- segment profitability canon
- managerial variance / responsibility view canon
- internal management reporting basis canon

## 3. Non-scope
It does not own:
- journal truth -> 30.accounting
- project financial canon -> 32.project-accounting
- cost truth -> 18.costing
- treasury execution -> 34.cash-management
