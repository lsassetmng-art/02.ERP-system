# PRINCIPLE 1

Dashboard is a derived surface, not a truth-owning layer.


# PRINCIPLE 2

Every dashboard tile should map back to at least one
exact underlying object family or derived dataset
with a stable interpretation path.


# PRINCIPLE 3

Dashboard should separate:
- account population
- segment distribution
- lifecycle distribution
- inquiry workload
- response timing
- owner load


# PRINCIPLE 4

A dashboard card may summarize many CRM objects,
but must not erase object-level ambiguity or delay.


# PRINCIPLE 5

Filtering and drill-down should lead to exact object
lists or exact analytical slices, not dead-end snapshots.
