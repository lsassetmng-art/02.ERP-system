# POSITIONING

CRM is a paid ERP option for richer customer management.

It is appropriate for organizations that want:

- customer account depth
- contact history
- inquiry continuity
- customer segmentation
- lifecycle visibility
- customer-side analysis


# NON POSITIONING

CRM is not the minimal requirement for all ERP users.

An organization that does not subscribe to CRM
should still be able to run core ERP flows that only
need lightweight customer reference.


# BUSINESS POSITION

CRM should be sold and activated as an optional
business module, not assumed as a universal default.
