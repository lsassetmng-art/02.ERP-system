# IN SCOPE

The paid CRM option may include:

- customer account detail
- contact/person records
- contact points
- interaction history
- inquiry and response history
- customer classification
- segmentation
- lifecycle state
- customer owner assignment
- customer-facing analysis surfaces


# OUT OF SCOPE FOR CORE

These are not required as universal ERP baseline objects.

They belong to the option side unless explicitly
promoted into another shared layer later.
