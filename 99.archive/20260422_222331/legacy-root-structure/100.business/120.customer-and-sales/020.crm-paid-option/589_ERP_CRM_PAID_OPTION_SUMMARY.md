# SUMMARY

CRM is now positioned as a paid ERP option,
not as mandatory ERP core foundation.

Main result:
- ERP core stays lighter
- CRM remains valuable and rich
- contracted organizations can enable it
- core transaction flows depend only on minimum
  customer reference, not on full CRM objects
