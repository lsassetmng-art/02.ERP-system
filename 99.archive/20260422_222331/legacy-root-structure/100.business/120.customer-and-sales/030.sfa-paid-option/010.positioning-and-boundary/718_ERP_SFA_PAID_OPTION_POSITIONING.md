# POSITIONING

SFA is the sales-execution and pipeline-management
paid option that sits on top of customer context
and around pre-order commercial activity.

Its design center is:
- lead handling
- opportunity progression
- next action planning
- sales forecast
- pipeline visibility
- sales workload oversight


# NON POSITIONING

SFA is not:
- the universal customer master
- the billing truth owner
- the order truth owner
- the inquiry continuity owner
- the warehouse or fulfillment layer
