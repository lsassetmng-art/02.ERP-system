# POSITIONING

SCM is the supply-chain coordination paid option that manages:
- supply network structure
- supplier and lane coordination
- cross-site replenishment interpretation
- transfer and replenishment recommendations
- disruption handling
- resilience visibility

Its design center is:
- network-wide coordination
- source and lane governance
- replenishment orchestration
- disruption visibility
- resilience assessment
- supply continuity planning


# NON POSITIONING

SCM is not:
- BOM-driven requirement planning owner
- warehouse location execution owner
- accounting valuation engine
- standalone transportation suite
- manufacturing floor execution layer
