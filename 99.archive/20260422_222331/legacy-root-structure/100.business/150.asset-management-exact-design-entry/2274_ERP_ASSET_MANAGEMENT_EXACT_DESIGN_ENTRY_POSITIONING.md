# POSITIONING

Asset management is the ERP-side long-lived asset lifecycle
and operational ownership truth line.

It is tightly adjacent to:
- accounting
- purchase
- management review and approval
- audit traceability

It is not merely an accounting appendix.
It is not inventory stock management.
