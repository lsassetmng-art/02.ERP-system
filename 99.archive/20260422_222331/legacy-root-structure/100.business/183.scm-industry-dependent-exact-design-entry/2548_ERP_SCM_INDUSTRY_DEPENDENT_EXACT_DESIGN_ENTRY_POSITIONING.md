# POSITIONING

SCM is the ERP-side cross-node supply orchestration,
supplier-customer network coordination,
transfer planning, and service-level exception module.

It is adjacent to:
- purchase and supply commitments
- sales demand visibility
- warehouse and inventory execution visibility
