# RULE

Inspection failure may justify nonconformance registration,
but inspection truth does not disappear.

Progression should preserve:
- linked inspection reference
- inspected item and lot visibility
- accepted and rejected quantity visibility
- discovered timestamp visibility
- failed or waived trace when applicable
