# POSITIONING

Cross-family linkage is the official ERP-side
contract and handoff line that connects:

- MRP planning
- manufacturing execution
- quality control
- warehouse execution
- supply coordination
- inventory visibility
- accounting posting handoff
- management review visibility

It does not replace each family's owned truth.
It defines only the official surfaces and transitions
between those owned truths.
