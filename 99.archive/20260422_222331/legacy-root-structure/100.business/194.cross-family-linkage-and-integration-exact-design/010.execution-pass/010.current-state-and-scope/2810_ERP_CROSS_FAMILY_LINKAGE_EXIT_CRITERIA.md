# EXIT CRITERIA

Cross-family linkage may be treated as execution-pass complete only when:
- official handoff surfaces are fixed
- event and request-response contracts are fixed
- lifecycle and retry semantics are fixed
- permission, screen, and reporting semantics are fixed
- implementation-ready checklist is passed
