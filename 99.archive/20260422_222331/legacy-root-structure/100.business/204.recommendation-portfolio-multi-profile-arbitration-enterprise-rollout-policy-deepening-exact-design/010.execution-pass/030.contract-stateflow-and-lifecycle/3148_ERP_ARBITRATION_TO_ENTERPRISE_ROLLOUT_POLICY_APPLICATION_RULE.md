# RULE

Arbitration outcome may justify enterprise rollout policy application,
but arbitration truth does not disappear.

Progression should preserve:
- winning profile visibility
- enterprise policy reference visibility
- exposure strategy visibility
- management visibility requirement
- rejected application trace when applicable
