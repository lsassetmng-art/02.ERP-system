# POSITIONING

Management core is the ERP oversight,
review, decision, and escalation truth layer.

It owns:
- management review-cycle meaning
- management decision truth
- escalation case truth
- action directive truth
- watchlist and issue visibility meaning

Management core does not own:
- operational business truth
- labor operation truth
- KPI target structure truth
- audit conclusion truth
