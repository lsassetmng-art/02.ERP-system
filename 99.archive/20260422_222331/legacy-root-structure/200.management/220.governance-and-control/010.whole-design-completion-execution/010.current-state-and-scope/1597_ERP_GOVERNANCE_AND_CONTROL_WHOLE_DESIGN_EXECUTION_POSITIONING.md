# POSITIONING

Governance/control is the ERP policy-enforcement
and control-assurance truth layer.

It owns:
- control policy truth
- control requirement truth
- governance exception truth
- override approval truth
- signoff truth

Governance/control does not own:
- operational business truth
- management decision truth
- labor operation truth
- KPI structure truth
- audit conclusion truth
