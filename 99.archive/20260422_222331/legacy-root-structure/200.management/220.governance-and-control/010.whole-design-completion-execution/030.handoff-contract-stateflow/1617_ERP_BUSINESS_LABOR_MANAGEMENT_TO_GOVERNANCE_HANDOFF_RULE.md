# RULE

Business core, LaborManager, and management core may hand off:
- control breach visibility
- exception trigger visibility
- policy update need visibility
- decision or directive context

Governance/control creates new owned truths:
- control policy
- control requirement
- exception case
- override approval
- signoff record
