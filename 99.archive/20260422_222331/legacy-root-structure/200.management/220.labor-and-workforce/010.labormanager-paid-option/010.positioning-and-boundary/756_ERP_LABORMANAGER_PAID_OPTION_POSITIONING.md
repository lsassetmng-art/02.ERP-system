# POSITIONING

LaborManager is the labor operations and workforce
governance paid option that manages employment-side
records, attendance-side records, leave workflows,
onboarding/offboarding handling, training progress,
and compliance visibility.

Its design center is:
- staff management
- employment continuity
- attendance governance
- leave governance
- onboarding/offboarding control
- training and compliance oversight


# NON POSITIONING

LaborManager is not:
- the shift allocation execution system
- the universal payroll engine
- a simple calendar-only app
- a pure recruiting system
