# POSITIONING

ShiftManager is the front-line allocation and roster
execution paid option that manages shift plans,
availability submissions, assignment decisions,
swap workflows, and final publication of working rosters.

Its design center is:
- shift planning
- availability and preference capture
- assignment execution
- swap handling
- roster publication
- staffing visibility


# NON POSITIONING

ShiftManager is not:
- employment truth owner
- attendance correction owner
- leave approval owner
- payroll calculation owner
- labor compliance truth owner
