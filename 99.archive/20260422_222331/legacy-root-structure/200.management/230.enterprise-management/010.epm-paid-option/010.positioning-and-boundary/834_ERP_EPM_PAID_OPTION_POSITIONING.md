# POSITIONING

EPM is the enterprise-management paid option that manages:
- company objectives
- department objectives
- KPI definitions
- planning cycles
- review cycles
- plan vs actual interpretation
- management scorecard surfaces

Its design center is:
- objective hierarchy
- KPI governance
- planning cadence
- review cadence
- variance interpretation
- management visibility


# NON POSITIONING

EPM is not:
- a lightweight personal goal tracker
- a generic BI-only shell
- a project task manager
- a substitute for CRM, SFA, LaborManager, or ShiftManager
