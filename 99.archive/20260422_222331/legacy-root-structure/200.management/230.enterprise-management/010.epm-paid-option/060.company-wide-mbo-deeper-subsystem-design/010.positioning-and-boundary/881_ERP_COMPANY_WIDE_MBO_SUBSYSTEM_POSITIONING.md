# POSITIONING

company_wide_mbo is the enterprise goal roll-down and
alignment subsystem inside EPM.

Its design center is:
- company objective roll-down
- department objective roll-down
- individual goal reference linkage
- progress check-in
- review sheet governance
- evaluation-support interpretation


# NON POSITIONING

company_wide_mbo is not:
- personal_mbo standalone product
- KPI definition framework itself
- review-cycle framework itself
- payroll or labor evaluation execution engine
