# POSITIONING

EPM is the ERP paid-option enterprise
objective, KPI, planning, and variance meaning layer.

It owns:
- objective set truth
- KPI metric truth
- scorecard snapshot truth
- planning/review cycle truth
- variance case truth

EPM does not own:
- management decision truth
- operational business truth
- accounting posting truth
- audit conclusion truth
- independent company_wide_mbo option truth
