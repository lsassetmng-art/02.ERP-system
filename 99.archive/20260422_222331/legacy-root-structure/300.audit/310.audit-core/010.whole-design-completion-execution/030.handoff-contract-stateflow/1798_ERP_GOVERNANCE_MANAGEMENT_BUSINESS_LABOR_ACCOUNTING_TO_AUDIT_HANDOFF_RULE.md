# RULE

Governance, management, business, labor, and accounting may hand off:
- control visibility
- decision or directive visibility
- operational evidence visibility
- labor evidence visibility
- posting and close visibility

Audit core creates new owned truths:
- audit engagement
- audit evidence item
- audit finding case
- audit conclusion record
- audit follow-up closure
