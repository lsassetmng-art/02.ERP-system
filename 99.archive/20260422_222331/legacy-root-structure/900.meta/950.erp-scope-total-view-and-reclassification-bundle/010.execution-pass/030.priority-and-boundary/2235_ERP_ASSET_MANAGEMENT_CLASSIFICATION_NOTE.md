# NOTE

Asset management is classified as ERP core,
not merely option or industry-dependent.

# REASON

Asset management is tightly adjacent to:
- accounting ownership
- depreciation and disposal control
- audit traceability
- long-lived operational ownership

Therefore it should be deepened before
many optional or industry-heavy extensions.
