# NOTE

Manufacturing management and its adjacent family:
- MRP
- WMS
- SCM
- quality management
- maintenance management
- cost management

are classified as ERP industry-dependent modules.

# REASON

They are legitimate ERP systems,
but their exact necessity and structure depend strongly on:
- manufacturing presence
- warehouse footprint
- plant operations
- quality and maintenance regime
