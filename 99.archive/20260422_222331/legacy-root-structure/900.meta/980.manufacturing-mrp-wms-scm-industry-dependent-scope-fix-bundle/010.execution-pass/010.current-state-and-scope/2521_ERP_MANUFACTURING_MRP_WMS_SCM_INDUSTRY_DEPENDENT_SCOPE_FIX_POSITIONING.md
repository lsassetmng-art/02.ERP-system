# POSITIONING

Manufacturing management, MRP, WMS, and SCM are:
- official ERP-side modules
- industry-dependent treatment modules
- closely adjacent to purchase, inventory, sales, accounting, and management
- not replacements for core purchase / inventory / accounting owned truths

Manufacturing management is the production execution line.
MRP is the material requirement and replenishment planning line.
WMS is the warehouse execution and warehouse-control line.
SCM is the cross-node supply orchestration and network coordination line.
