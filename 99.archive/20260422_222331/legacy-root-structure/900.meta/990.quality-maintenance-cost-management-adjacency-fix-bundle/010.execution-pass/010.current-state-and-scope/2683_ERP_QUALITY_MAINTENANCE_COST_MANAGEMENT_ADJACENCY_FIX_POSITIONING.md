# POSITIONING

Quality management, maintenance management,
and manufacturing cost management are:
- official ERP-side adjacent modules
- tightly adjacent to manufacturing execution
- not replacements for manufacturing / inventory / accounting / asset truths

Quality management is the inspection and nonconformance line.
Maintenance management is the equipment-maintenance and downtime line.
Manufacturing cost management is the cost collection and variance line.
