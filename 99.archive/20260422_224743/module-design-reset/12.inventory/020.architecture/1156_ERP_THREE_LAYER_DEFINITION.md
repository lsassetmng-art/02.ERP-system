# THREE LAYERS

Base layer:
- foundational rules
- authority
- audit
- shared structure
- shared contract principles
- navigation / index / ownership rules

Business layer:
- ERP core business operation lines
- sales
- inventory
- accounting
- purchasing
- manufacturing
- related operational business structures

Management layer:
- governance and management interpretation
- management controls
- EPM
- company_wide_mbo
- management reporting and review structures
